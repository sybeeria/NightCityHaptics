In game

Open the CET overlay for a small Night City Haptics window with Pause and Test buzz.

For hotkeys, bind Pause / resume haptics and Test buzz under CET → Bindings.

Output only flows during gameplay: menus, pause, loading screens, photo mode and the CET overlay are always silent.

Defaults

Put every interaction back to its default on/off, strength and feel. Device settings and Intiface options stay.

For mod authors

Other CET mods can send a pulse through the Other mods interaction:

GetMod("nightCityHaptics").Pulse(0.8, 2)

Strength 0–1, length in seconds (optional).
