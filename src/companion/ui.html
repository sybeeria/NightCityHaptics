Intiface

In game

Open the CET overlay for Pause and Test buzz, or bind both as hotkeys in CET’s Bindings. Menus, loading screens and photo mode stay silent.

Defaults

Puts every interaction back to its default switch, feel and strength.

Other mods

CET mods can pulse through the Other mods interaction (strength 0–1, seconds optional):

GetMod("nightCityHaptics").Pulse(0.8, 2)

Interactions

Changes save as you make them. The app keeps running until you press Quit.

Night City Haptics is not running

Start it again with start.cmd on Windows or start.sh on Linux, in the mod’s companion folder, or with your shortcut. This page reconnects by itself.
